# Background and Motivation 

The NASA Science Mission Directorate (SMD) policy [SPD-41a: Scientific Information Policy for the Science Mission Directorate](https://science.nasa.gov/spd-41/) provides requirements and guidelines on openly sharing scientific publications, data, and software created in the pursuit of scientific knowledge.  SPD-41a builds upon the core principles of openness and security for SMD-funded scientific activities. Implementation of SPD-41a is one component of SMD's [broader initiative](https://science.nasa.gov/open-science/) to increase the transparency, availability, and reproducibility of NASA science through the adoption of open science practices.  [SPD-41a](https://science.nasa.gov/spd-41/) updates the previously released [SPD-41](https://smd-cms.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ScientificInformationpolicySPD_41.pdf), which consolidated existing Federal and NASA policy on sharing scientific information.  

## Scope and Purpose of this Document 

SPD-41a applies to SMD-funded scientific activities. This includes
SMD-funded flight missions that had not yet reached the Key Decision
Point-B (KDP-B) milestone as of March 2023. Missions that passed KDP-B
prior to March 2023 are encouraged to adopt the requirements of SPD-41a
if consistent with available resources.   Previous flight missions will
still have requirements related to open science that pre-date SPD-41a,
and they may still find the guidance in this document helpful.   

This document provides guidelines, best practices, and examples of open
science to support SMD-funded flight missions in implementing the
requirements of SPD-41a. This guidance represents general
recommendations and best practices for the SMD scientific community and
should be considered alongside any additional guidance provided by SMD
divisions or specific funding solicitations. For additional information
about SPD-41a and other scientific information policies that correspond
to SMD Divisions, see the [Scientific Information Policy website](https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/science-data/science-information-policy)
and [SPD-41a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/science-data/science-information-policy_faq).


## Limitations

This document does not establish policy requirements related to SMD funded activities. While this guidance is consistent with SPD-41a and other NASA policies, it may not be comprehensive or complete, and there may be other ways in which to comply with existing or future agreements or requirements not described here. For specific requirements related to scientific information produced from SMD-funded scientific activities, please see SPD-41a, NASA policies, and/or any requirements in the solicitation or agreement associated with the activity. 

This guidance is focused on SMD-funded components of SMD-led flight missions. SPD-41a includes additional requirements and best practices for SMD’s participation in missions with its partners. Partnerships are not covered in this current version of the guidance. 

## Contributions

This guidance will be developed as new information becomes available, with future updates made openly on the [Open Science Guidance GitHub repository](https://github.com/nasa/smd-open-science-guidelines). Following the best practices of open science, this material can be best built through the collective knowledge of the community. To report something that needs to be fixed or to suggest an addition, please contribute to the GitHub repository or send an email to [HQ-SMD-SPD41@mail.nasa.gov](mailto:HQ-SMD-SPD41@mail.nasa.gov).

## Additional Resources

### Scientific Information Website

The most up to date information on the scientific information policies and SPD-41a can be found on the [Scientific Information Policy webpage](https://science.nasa.gov/sip).  This includes the latest version of the policy, frequently asked questions, and links to other resources including the data polices for each division.  

### Open Science Guidance for Researchers

The [Open-Source Science Guidance for SMD-funded Researchers](https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/science-information-policy/#oss-guidance) provides related information on implementing SPD-41a requirements, tailored for SMD Research and Analysis (R&A) proposers and awardees. 

### Open Science 101

[Open Science 101](https://science.nasa.gov/open-science/os101/) provides a general introduction for scientific audiences  to open science principles and practices. The Open Science 101 curriculum complements this guidance by providing additional definitions, tools, resources, and best practices.
